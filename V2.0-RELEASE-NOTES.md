# MCW v2.0.0 Release Notes

Last updated: March 27, 2026

## Release Meaning

`v2.0.0` marks the point where Modular Cryptography Workbench becomes a coherent **cryptographic systems IDE** rather than an expanding collection of features.

This release is not defined by:
- one new primitive family
- a technology rewrite
- a single dramatic UI change

It is defined by the product reaching an intentional and aligned form across:
- machine vocabulary
- teaching progression
- primitive discovery
- workbench usability

## What `v2.0.0` Represents

MCW now provides:
- a broad explicit-machine language for symbolic, bitwise, modern, and systems-composition teaching
- a staged learning surface that carries users from foundational concepts into integrity, authentication, signatures, and handshake composition
- a product-shaped primitive palette rather than a flat engine inventory
- a workbench that remains usable and orientable even in larger system demos

In plain terms:
- MCW now has the shape of its first mature product identity

## Cohesion Arc That Led Here

The post-`v1.44.0` convergence line established the `v2.0.0` boundary:
- `v1.45.0` — `LEARNING-SEQUENCE-V2`
- `v1.46.0` — learning-path selector polish
- `v1.47.0` — `PALETTE-COHERENCE-V1`
- `v1.48.0` — large-workspace orientation support via Workspace Landmarks

Together, those slices resolved the three major product-shape pressures that had emerged:
- learning-library hierarchy
- primitive palette coherence
- large-workspace orientation

## What Did Not Change

`v2.0.0` does **not** mean:
- the roadmap is complete
- no future work remains
- MCW is frozen in place

It means future work now starts from a clearer, more coherent base.

## Post-`v2.0.0` Direction

The next question is not “how do we finally get to `2.0`?”

The next question is:
- what is the first bounded post-`v2.0` milestone that meaningfully improves the product without breaking the coherence just established?

That is the starting point for `v2.1`.
