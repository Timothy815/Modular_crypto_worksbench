# INSTRUCTOR-PILOT-PACK-V1

Status: Implemented

Owner: Codex
Scope: Instructor Resources / Pilot Guidance / Adoption Support

## Why

MCW now has enough shipped product surface that the next adoption bottleneck is not capability.

The next bottleneck is instructor confidence:
- what should I run first?
- what can students realistically do in one class?
- what makes MCW special compared with existing tools?
- how do I use verification and export without derailing the lesson?

The right next move is one bounded instructor-facing pilot pack that turns the shipped flagship labs into a usable teaching plan.

## Goal

Provide one instructor-facing resource inside MCW that helps a professor or teacher evaluate the product quickly, choose a first pilot path, and understand what evidence to look for during student use.

## Product Boundary

This slice is:
- instructor-facing
- adoption-facing
- built from already-shipped flagship labs, manual, verification, and export surfaces

It is not:
- a full LMS integration
- a grading engine
- a course-management system
- a broad curriculum rewrite

## Required V1 Shape

1. Add an `Instructor Pilot Pack` resource entry alongside the existing `User Manual` and `AI Toolkit`.
2. Open the pack in a dedicated resource window, not as a detached panel kind.
3. Support:
   - table of contents
   - search
   - index
4. Cover at least:
   - what makes MCW distinctive
   - which flagship lab to start with
   - one-class pilot suggestions
   - what students should notice
   - how verification/export fit into the session
   - what feedback an instructor should collect
5. Keep the pack concise and practical rather than turning it into a full instructor handbook.

## Success Condition

This slice is successful if an instructor can open MCW, read one resource, and answer:
- what should I try first?
- what makes this tool different?
- what should my students do?
- what should I watch for during a pilot?
