# V2.0 Readiness

Last updated: March 27, 2026

Status: Completed. This contract concluded that MCW had reached a real `v2.0` readiness decision surface.

## Purpose

This contract defines the next bounded project-level decision slice after the recent cohesion arc.

The goal is not to add another feature by default.
The goal is to decide whether MCW is now ready for a real `v2.0` boundary, and if not, to identify the single clearest remaining blocker.

## Why Now

The recent post-`v1.44.0` cohesion line is now substantially complete:
- `LEARNING-SEQUENCE-V2`
- selector/pathfinding polish
- `PALETTE-COHERENCE-V1`
- `LARGE-WORKSPACE-UX-TRIAGE-V1`

Those slices addressed the three pressure points identified by the earlier sanity work:
- learning-library hierarchy
- palette/category coherence
- large-workspace usability

That means the next unresolved question is no longer:
- what local polish slice should we do next?

It is now:
- has MCW reached the level of coherence and completeness that justifies `v2.0`?

## Core Question

This slice should answer:
- is MCW now ready to be framed as `v2.0`?

If the answer is no, this slice should answer:
- what one missing condition most clearly prevents that framing?

## Scope

This contract is limited to:
- reviewing the current product state after `v1.48.0`
- reviewing the recent cohesion arc as a whole
- defining what `v2.0` would mean for MCW
- determining whether MCW already satisfies that threshold
- naming at most one remaining blocker if the threshold is not yet met

This slice may produce:
- a readiness decision
- a short readiness/audit document
- a recommendation for either:
  - declaring `v2.0`
  - or one final bounded pre-`v2.0` milestone

- overall product framing as a cryptographic systems IDE

## Product Goal

`v2.0` should mean more than:
- elapsed time
- feature count
- “we have been on `v1.x` for a while”

`v2.0` should mark that MCW has become:
- coherent
- intentional
- strategically legible
- stable enough in product shape to stand as the next major release boundary

## Non-Goals

Do not use this slice to:
- invent a broad speculative roadmap
- add new primitives by default
- force a `v2.0` label just because the number feels overdue
- reopen already-completed cohesion work without concrete evidence
- start a broad redesign during the readiness decision itself

## Required Behaviors

1. **Define the standard before judging against it**
- explicitly state what `v2.0` is supposed to mean for MCW

2. **Prefer one blocker over a wishlist**
- if MCW is not yet ready, name the single most important missing condition

3. **Protect against semantic inflation**
- do not call it `v2.0` unless there is a real product boundary worth naming

4. **Protect against endless deferral**
- do not keep MCW in indefinite late `v1.x` unless there is a concrete and defensible blocker

5. **Use product judgment, not version superstition**
- the recommendation should be grounded in product coherence, not in abstract semver aesthetics

## Success Criteria

This contract is successful when:
- it defines a clear meaning for `v2.0`
- it determines whether MCW now satisfies that meaning
- it avoids both premature promotion and endless drift
- it produces either:
  - a justified `v2.0` readiness decision
  - or one bounded final pre-`v2.0` milestone recommendation

## Suggested Validation Questions

Review the current project by asking:
- does MCW now feel like a coherent product rather than an accumulated feature set?
- are the key product surfaces aligned with each other?
- is there a major remaining mismatch between machine capability and product framing?
- would calling the current form of MCW `v2.0` clarify the project, or overstate it?
- if `v2.0` would be premature, what exactly is still missing?

## Likely Deliverable

The output of this contract should likely be:
- `V2.0-READINESS-AUDIT.md`

That deliverable should conclude one of three things:
- MCW is ready for `v2.0`
- MCW is not ready, and one final bounded milestone should happen first
- MCW should remain on `v1.x`, with a clearly stated reason
