# STRUCTURED-EDITOR-UNIFICATION-V1

## Goal

Make structured primitive editors feel like one family without rewriting their core behaviors.

## Shipped Shape

- Shared meta-band treatment across:
  - S-box
  - permutation
  - rotor
  - reflector
  - plugboard
- Shared utility-card treatment for calmer helper sections.
- Shared quieter helper/explanatory copy treatment.
- Reflector and plugboard utility areas now sit in the same visual language as the newer S-box and permutation editor surfaces.

## Boundaries

- No primitive semantics changed.
- No authoring workflows were removed.
- This pass is visual/systemic only.

## Notes

- The intent is coherence, not forced symmetry.
- Reflector and plugboard remain simpler than S-box and permutation because their direct-manipulation surfaces were already adequate.
