# SYSTEMS-IDE-COHERENCE-V1

Last updated: March 28, 2026

Status: Proposed

---

## Purpose

Define a bounded north-star coherence pass for MCW now that:
- Python export has reached its first real completeness and productization milestone
- multi-window has become a capable bounded workbench surface
- the first major `v2.1` builder-power line is materially advanced

This slice is not about adding another major feature family.
It is about deciding what MCW most clearly is now, what the product should emphasize, and what should be deliberately deferred to preserve shape.

---

## Problem

MCW is no longer suffering from a lack of capability.

The risk has changed.

The main danger now is:
- broad capability without enough product-shape consolidation
- too many strong lines competing for equal attention
- future work being chosen by local attractiveness rather than product identity

If left unattended, that can produce a tool that is individually impressive but less legible as one coherent product.

---

## Strategic Position

MCW should now be treated as a **cryptographic systems IDE**.

That identity is stronger than:
- visual crypto sandbox
- simulator collection
- teaching toy

It implies a product with a stable center:
- explicit machine authoring
- guided understanding
- serious workbench ergonomics
- export to executable artifacts

This coherence pass should make those pillars explicit and use them to judge what comes next.

---

## Required V1 Shape

This slice must:
- state clearly what MCW most clearly is now
- identify the strongest current product pillars
- identify the main strategic risk at the current stage
- define the canonical workflows that best represent MCW at its strongest
- distinguish:
  - core product surfaces
  - supporting surfaces
  - tempting but deferrable branches
- recommend the best next move in terms of product coherence, not novelty

It must not:
- become a giant roadmap rewrite
- flatten all future work into one mega-plan
- invent speculative new product directions disconnected from the current repo state
- silently reprioritize active work without giving explicit reasons

---

## Working Thesis

The current best product reading is:

MCW is a visual cryptographic systems IDE for building, understanding, arranging, and exporting explicit cryptographic machines.

The likely product pillars under that thesis are:
- Author
  - build explicit machines from visible parts
- Understand
  - inspect, trace, compare, and learn from live behavior
- Arrange
  - use the workspace and detached windows as a real working environment
- Export
  - carry machines out as executable Python artifacts

This pass exists to either confirm that framing or replace it with a stronger one.

---

## Deliverable

This slice should produce one bounded strategic note that answers:
- What MCW most clearly is now
- What its strongest current product pillars are
- What the main strategic risk is at this stage
- Whether the best next move is:
  - further feature expansion
  - product coherence / consolidation
  - deployment / user feedback
  - or something else
- What one or two next actions best preserve product identity

The deliverable should be short, explicit, and decision-oriented.

---

## Success Condition

This slice is successful when:
- MCW’s current product identity is stated more clearly than “a lot of cool crypto features”
- the strongest pillars are named and bounded
- the main strategic risk is identified honestly
- the next move is justified against product coherence rather than novelty
- future prioritization can use this pass as a north-star check without reopening the entire roadmap
